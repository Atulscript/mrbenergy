About MRB Energy Solutions

Pan-India Solar EPC & Engineering

From residential rooftops to commercial organizations and industrial factories, MRB Energy delivers turnkey solar engineering across India.

Engineering-First Solar EPC

Founded by certified renewable energy engineers with over 15+ years of extensive commercial and industrial solar EPC execution experience, MRB Energy Solutions was built to deliver dependable, high-efficiency solar power to homes, small businesses, and manufacturing plants.

We manage the full project lifecycle: on-site feasibility audits, 3D shadow analysis, single-line diagram (SLD) engineering, liaisoning with state electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) across India, Central Govt PM Surya Ghar subsidy disbursement, and 25-year lifecycle performance monitoring.
