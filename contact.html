A bidirectional net meter records both imported grid electricity and exported solar surplus. At the end of each billing cycle, you only pay for the net difference. If you generate surplus, it credits to your next bill.
Commercial solar systems qualify for a 40% depreciation rate in Year 1 under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act. For profitable companies paying 25.17% corporate tax, this directly reduces income tax liability by ~10–15% of project cost.
